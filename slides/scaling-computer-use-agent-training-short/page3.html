Personal intro

Bowen Wang

I'm a 2nd year Ph.D. student at the University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Tao Yu. Prev@Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on building universal digital agents. I have primarily worked on computer-use agents (CUA), developing open foundations through mid-training and post-training and building open-sourced CUA models.

My current research interests lie in digital agentic models, particularly in post-training methodologies for creating generalizable and intelligent agents that can reliably operate across diverse digital environments including coding, computer-use, terminal-use and more.

I hope to make computer-use a core capability of frontier models. I believe universal digital agents (UDA) are a path toward AGI.

CUA-Gym: Environment Scaling
Three limitations stand in the way of training computer-use agents at scale.
We build CUA-Gym-Hub: 98 self-contained mock applications across 7 domains — all state-injectable and parallel-isolated.
(1)
OSWorld & WebArena cover only limited applications
drifting further from the authentic domains where computer-use agents are expected to operate.
(2)
Agents need to transfer quickly to unseen domains and apps
so the training distribution has to cover the long tail, not just a few canonical desktop tools.
(3)
Real apps can’t be deployed as RL environments
they are not sandboxes — no state injection, no parallel isolation, no resetability.

What's next

CUA-Pro and UDA.

01

CUA-Pro: professional and long-horizon tasks

Computer-use agents need to move beyond short desktop chores into professional work: designing, multimedia editing, business operations, finance analysis, and other long-horizon workflows.

02

UDA-Gym: seamless CUA + coding harness

The next training environment should fuse GUI interaction with coding harnesses, terminal tools, tests, and verifiers. UDA-Gym creates verifiable tasks and environments for professional CUA and unified digital agents.

From computer-use agents to unified digital agents.
